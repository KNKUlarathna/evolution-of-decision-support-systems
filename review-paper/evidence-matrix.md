# Source evidence matrix

This is the extraction record for a critical narrative review, not an independently screened systematic-review dataset. Entries identify the inspected portions and bounded use of each source. Cross-generation causal connections and the five-level evaluation framework are the manuscript’s synthesis or proposal. No causal effect sizes were estimated.

The catalog contains 67 records and 42 direct PDF links; 38 PDF files were obtained. The manuscript cites 24 selected records: 23 have extractable local article text; DSS022 has publisher metadata but image-based article pages. Counts are file/record counts, not deduplicated study counts. Recent catalog metadata was not assumed valid merely because a file downloaded.

## Historical and bibliographic limitations

Early interactive, group and Web DSS claims primarily use Shim et al. and Power as historical syntheses. The review does not claim firsthand assessment of every early primary source. References to preprints identify the checked arXiv work rather than asserting a separately verified final venue. Conference-year and arXiv-version dates may differ. DSS022 uses the publisher title “Note—A Preference Ranking Organisation Method,” correcting the expanded descriptive catalog title in the manuscript citation. Final reference-by-reference editorial verification remains a submission task.

## Reproduction

Source PDFs are in the repository’s `DSS_PDFs/` directory, grouped by category. Text extraction for this draft used `pdftotext -layout`; it does not provide reliable extraction from every scanned page. The paper introduces no new numerical experiment.
