## Corpus boundary

Full text available means the complete text was obtained, with relevant argument/method/results/limitation sections assessed. It does not imply every sentence was independently checked. Three theory scans required OCR; two MIT working papers are cited as the actual versions read. The initial 67-record catalog was a discovery pool, not a completed systematic screening population. Abstract/metadata-only candidates in `excluded-candidates.json` were not counted in the 24-work substantive synthesis.

## Claim-level traceability

The manuscript’s evidence-to-mechanism table identifies directional links, page/section locations, evidence types and counterconditions. `claim-evidence.md` further distinguishes direct support from cross-study inference. No source directly establishes the entire long-run historical feedback loop.
