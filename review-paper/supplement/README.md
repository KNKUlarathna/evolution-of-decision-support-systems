# Research supplement

The folders retain actual source manifests, page/section-located extraction notes and available search/retrieval/selection logs from 21 October 2026. This record documents targeted theory-guided work, not a comprehensive search of all literature.

- [Working protocol](review-protocol.md): written during research before manuscript assembly; not preregistered.
- [Organizational theory](theory/evidence.md): uncertainty, interdependence, equivocality, scarce attention and balancing responses.
- [Historical DSS](history/evidence.md): assessed primary working papers, classifications, knowledge engineering, warehousing and knowledge discovery.
- [Feedback and production learning](feedback/evidence.md): formal/simulation mechanisms, correction conditions, engineering taxonomy and organizational case evidence.
- [Clinical implementation](integration/evidence.md): Sutton and Greenhalgh full-text evidence and access-limited candidates.
- [Technical mechanisms](technical/evidence.md): distributed execution, retrieval and tool-action loops, without treating them as demonstrated adoption generations.
- [Prior-review positioning](positioning/evidence.md): exact overlap, limits and comparison with existing reviews.

Each folder's `sources.json` is a research-stage record and may include context-only leads or aliases. The authoritative deduplicated 24-work included corpus is `../source-manifest.json`. `../selection-register.csv` distinguishes included sources from explicitly considered but unassessed candidates. No PRISMA flow or exhaustive screening denominator is inferred from these files. Other records in the original 67-entry catalog were discovery context, not implicitly screened studies.

Some logs contain failed 403/429 requests, irrelevant search results or rejected repository matches. These are retained as evidence of access/selection limitations. Working copies may have been retrieved previously and reassessed for this revision; source provenance distinguishes source URLs from local paths. Public availability does not itself establish redistribution rights.
