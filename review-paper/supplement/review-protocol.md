# Working review protocol

Dated 21 October 2026. This working protocol was written during research, before manuscript assembly; it is not a preregistered systematic-review protocol.

Question: How has DSS design co-evolved with the feedback between organizational complexity and growing data, and under what conditions do decision-support interventions reinforce or dampen that relationship?

Review type: critical integrative review with purposive, theory-guided sampling. No exhaustive database coverage, PRISMA compliance, independent double screening or pooled causal effects are claimed.

Source routes: existing 67-record DSS catalog; targeted scholarly-metadata queries in Crossref; authoritative publisher/author/repository full texts; reference-driven selection from foundational works. Record actual query outcomes (including rate limits), not hypothetical searches.

Eligibility: a source must materially support an organizational-information-processing mechanism, an actual DSS/BI architectural response, an implementation outcome, an endogenous-data/feedback mechanism, or a directly comparable prior review. English-language works published by 21 October 2026. Historical originals retained regardless of date. Quantitative benchmark papers alone cannot establish a DSS historical generation. Use full text for substantive synthesis. Abstract/metadata-only candidates remain contextual leads unless clearly marked and excluded from claim support.

Assessment: identify design and setting (conceptual, mathematical, simulation, empirical organizational, technical architecture, secondary synthesis), observed versus proposed mechanism, page/section-located evidence, competing explanation, and transfer limits. Read relevant argument/results/limitations, not merely abstracts. Grade claims by evidence type rather than constructing a spurious aggregate quality score.

Synthesis: compare DSS configurations at the common level of their dominant organizational information-processing arrangement. Components such as RAG are mechanisms within configurations, not automatically independent historical generations. Separate data volume from variety, timeliness, quality, and access; separate task uncertainty from equivocality and interdependence. Trace individual arrows in a conditional feedback model and label original cross-study inferences. Include balancing responses and counterexamples.

Deliverables: IEEE-style manuscript, PDF, reading copy, source manifest, evidence/claim matrices, retrieval/search log, screening decisions and comparison with closest reviews. Do not present downloaded file counts as study counts or accessibility as source quality. Verify references and PDF/citation rendering; independently audit the final argument before push.
