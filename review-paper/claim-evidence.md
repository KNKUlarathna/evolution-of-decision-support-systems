## Assessment process

Page/section extracts were checked in separate research passes; internal AI-assisted argument reviews then identified and corrected: gross-versus-net work conflation; policy-dependent versus statistically dependent observations; assumptions about resident reporting; missing volume-demand pathway in the figure; and over-broad balancing arrows. This was not external peer review or independent human validation. See `validation.md` for completed document checks and remaining boundaries.
